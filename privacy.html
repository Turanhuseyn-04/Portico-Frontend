What Portico keeps

This notice covers the site you are using now. A reservation here is a preview. Nothing is charged, and no account is stored on a server.

What this version stores

The site does not send your name, email, or stay to a server. A stay you save with the heart is kept in this browser only. Dates and guests you choose are written into the page address, so a link you copy carries that search with it.

Clearing this browser's saved data removes the stays you marked.

A reservation preview

Confirm reservation asks for a name, an email, an optional phone number, and an optional note for the property. The form stays in the browser long enough to show a reference number. It is not a booking, it is not emailed, and it is not kept after you leave the page.

When booking is real

A later version will store the guest's name, email, phone if you give one, the note you wrote, the property, the room, the dates, the number of guests, and the stay total. Portico will use that to hold the stay and to send the confirmation. Portico will not sell it.
