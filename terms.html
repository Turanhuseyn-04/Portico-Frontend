Before you book

The terms of a Portico stay

Portico keeps a short list of stays. These terms say what a price means, and what a reservation on this site does today.

The list

If a stay is not on the list, Portico is not offering it. The list, the photographs, and the prices can change. A house that cannot sleep your group stays on the list and says so.

The price

The stay total is the nightly rate times the number of nights times the number of rooms. The card states that this total includes taxes and charges. A price shown before you add dates is a nightly rate, not a total for a stay.

Cancellation

Where a room offers free cancellation, it runs until three days before check-in. A room that does not offer it is marked non-refundable. Some stays ask for a minimum number of nights. The property page states the rule that applies to that stay.

A reservation on this site

Confirm reservation does not hold a room and does not take payment. The reference it shows is a preview. A stay is booked only when Portico confirms it and a payment step exists.

The house

The house rules on the property page are part of that stay: check-in, check-out, children, pets, and quiet hours. You agree to those rules when a booking is later confirmed.

The account

Sign in and register on this site store nothing. They show the door an account will use later. See the privacy notice for what is kept in this browser, and what a later version will store.
