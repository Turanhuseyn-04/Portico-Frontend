Before you book

Help with a stay

The short answers live here. The dates, the price, and the house rules for one stay are on that stay's page.

Finding a stay

Search by city, dates, and guests. The list stays short. A house that cannot sleep your group remains on the list and says so.

The price

Add check-in and check-out, then choose a room. The stay total is the nightly rate times the number of nights times the number of rooms. That total includes the taxes and charges stated on the card. A price shown before you add dates is a nightly rate, not a total.

Check-in and check-out

Each stay prints its own hours. A hotel on this list usually checks in from 15:00 and checks out by 11:00. Read the house rules on the stay before you rely on those hours.

Cancellation

Where a room offers free cancellation, it runs until three days before check-in. A room that does not offer it is marked non-refundable. Some stays ask for a minimum number of nights. The property page states the rule that applies.

A reservation on this site

Confirm reservation does not hold a room and does not take payment. The reference it shows is a preview. Nothing is emailed. The terms and the privacy notice say what that preview stores.
