How the price works

Three rules, said out loud

01

The total is nights times rate

Search with dates and a card switches from a nightly rate to the stay total. The sum is on the card before you continue.

02

Cancellation is a date

Free cancellation runs until three days before check-in, and only on houses that offer it. Others say non-refundable.

03

If it cannot sleep you, it says so

A house stays on the list when the group is too large. It does not pretend the room still fits.
